"In the beginning I looked around and could not find the dream car I dreamed of, so I decided to build it myself." Ferry Porsche's founding statement isn't about cars. It's about the gap between imagination and material reality—and the conviction to close it. For Porsche's 75th anniversary, Wallpaper\* commissioned a short film interpreting this philosophy for installation at Outernet London, a five-surface LED architecture forming an immersive cube in the city's theatre district. Incomplete Infinity led creative direction and production through Lusion, with two and a half weeks from concept to delivery.

</MdxParagraph>

<MdxParagraph>

A heritage piece showing archival footage and iconic silhouettes would satisfy the brief. It wouldn't honour the idea. The narrative structure moved from abstract to concrete—luminous particles converging into the Porsche crest, energy coalescing into form, a seed emerging and blossoming into shapes that gradually reveal seven decades of the marque's lineage. The arc embodies Ferry Porsche's philosophy rather than illustrating it: dreams becoming material, imagination taking shape, the passage from possibility to presence.

</MdxParagraph>

<MdxParagraph>

The venue demanded architectural thinking. Five surfaces surrounding viewers meant the work couldn't be composed for a single frame—it had to move through space, holding coherence as visitors walked through the installation. The compressed timeline required continuous production across three time zones, with creative decisions that would normally unfold over weeks compressed into hours. The result played at Outernet for the anniversary programme, translating a founder's conviction into an environment visitors could inhabit.

</MdxParagraph>
